# Getting help with Noop Ridge

- **🐞 Bugs → open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/eccentricmeerkat/noopridge/issues/new).** Include your **Noop version** (Hub › About), your **strap model**, and a **strap log**. Noop runs entirely on your device, so without one it is usually impossible to tell a real fault from a setup detail. In the app: **Settings → Strap → "Share strap log (for bug reports)"**. Check the log for anything you do not want to share before posting it.
- **📖 Docs → the [README](README.md)** covers install (Android Play Protect), pairing a WHOOP 5.0/MG, privacy, and importing your history. Build steps are in [`android/README.md`](android/README.md).
- **🌱 The original project** this build is derived from is [github.com/ryanbr/noop](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop); its wiki and discussions cover the shared protocol and strap behaviour.

Noop is an independent, unofficial project: **not affiliated with WHOOP, Inc.**, and **not a medical device**.
