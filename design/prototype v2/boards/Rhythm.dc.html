Before you turn on Rhythm

Rhythm plots the gap between each heartbeat overnight to show how regular your rhythm looks. It uses the strap's optical sensor.

  • It is not a diagnosis and cannot detect any condition.
  • It is not an ECG. Optical readings miss and misplace beats.
  • If you have symptoms, talk to a doctor, whatever this shows.
